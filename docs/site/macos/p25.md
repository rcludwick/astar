# Listening to P25 reflectors

P25 is new in astar, and it is **in testing**. It first ships in 0.1.25-beta,
and for now astar can only **listen** to P25: you can join a reflector and
hear the net, but you cannot key up. The network picker marks P25 "In
testing" so nobody mistakes it for a finished feature.

Here is what you do. In the network picker, choose **P25**. Type a talkgroup
number, or search the directory, and press **Connect**. A few seconds later
the status card shows the net, and the speaker starts playing whoever is
talking.

## Dialling a P25 reflector

A **reflector** is a server on the internet that repeats every station's
audio to everyone else who has joined it. P25 reflectors are run by hobbyists
and clubs, and astar lists them in its directory.

There are two ways to pick one:

- **From the directory.** Click the magnifying glass beside the dial field
  and search, as described in [Searching the reflector
  directory](reflectors.md). P25 rows are listed beside the other networks.
  Click one and astar fills in the field for you.
- **By typing an address.** Type `host`, `host:port`, or either of those
  followed by `/` and a talkgroup number, for example
  `p25.example.org:41000/10100`. If you leave the port off, astar uses
  **41000**, the usual P25 reflector port.

A **talkgroup** is a numbered channel that radios choose on their side. On
some other networks a reflector carries many talkgroups at once. A P25
reflector carries just one, so astar never sends the talkgroup anywhere. It
only shows the number you typed, as a label, so you can see what you meant to
join. The directory rows that have no number, such as a parrot, work the same
way: astar dials the address and shows no label.

P25 needs a callsign, which astar sends to the reflector so it knows who has
joined. It is the callsign you set in Settings, and it can be up to ten
characters. If it is too long, astar says so before it dials.

## What you will see

While you are connected, the status card shows **In testing** beside the P25
badge, and under it a line for the station that spoke last, such as:

> Last heard 1234567 · 4 s

That number is the talker's **radio ID**, the number programmed into their
radio. astar shows the number rather than a callsign for now, the same way it
does for DMR and NXDN. The level meters and the heard list work as they do on
the other networks.

### Encrypted calls stay silent

Some P25 users encrypt their voice, so that only radios with the right key
can understand it. astar cannot decrypt a call and never tries. It has no
code for it at all. When it sees that a call is encrypted, it plays nothing
and records nothing, and the talker line ends with **Encrypted** so you know
why you hear silence:

> Last heard 1234567 · 4 s · Encrypted

Amateur radio rules forbid encrypted transmissions on the air, so on an
amateur reflector you should rarely see this.

### If the link drops

A reflector that stops answering for two minutes counts as lost. The status
card then reads **P25 link lost**, with a **Reconnect** button that dials the
same reflector again.

## Receive only for now

You cannot transmit on P25 yet. The push-to-talk button is refused with a
reason, and the card says "Receive only for now" under the picker. Sending
P25 voice is planned, and it will be added in a later release. Until then
the network can only be listened to.

## No dongle needed

The other digital voice networks that astar reaches with a dongle (D-Star,
System Fusion, NXDN and DMR) turn voice into sound with a small chip on a USB
stick. P25 does not use one. astar decodes P25's voice, called **IMBE**, in
software on your Mac, so there is nothing to buy or plug in. The DVSI sticks
that work on the other networks cannot do P25's full-rate voice anyway.

The software decoder is a port of the one in the open-source OP25 project.
Because of its licence, astar keeps it in a clearly separate part of the
source. [Settings → Vocoders](vocoders.md) shows a P25 row reading **Software
(IMBE)**, with nothing to choose. In a build of astar made without P25, the
row reads **Not in this build**, and P25 rows in the directory are greyed out
with that reason.

## Recording and the packet log

[Recording](recording.md) and the [packet log](packet-log.md) work on P25 as
they do on the other networks. Encrypted calls are never recorded.
